5Deal registration & attribution

5.1  Submission.

Partner shall submit each Deal Registration via the Partner Portal, identifying the prospective Customer's legal name, primary contact, and engagement context. Registrations submitted by any other means (email, slack, verbal) are not binding.

5.2  Acceptance.

Company shall communicate acceptance, rejection, or co-sell proposal within two (2) business days. Acceptance creates an attribution record in favor of Partner.

5.2.1  Provisional acceptance & verification.

Because Company responds within the two (2) business-day window of §5.2 and §4.4, an acceptance is provisional until Company has completed its duplicate, Customer-identity, and eligibility checks against the criteria in §5.4. During the provisional period Company may begin engaging the Customer without prejudice to attribution, and no Commission vests on a provisional registration. If those checks show the registration is void under §5.4 — including that it duplicates an earlier valid registration or misstates the Customer's identity — Company may revoke the provisional acceptance on written notice, and attribution is determined under §5.6 and §5.7. Provisional acceptance becomes final when Company confirms the registration has cleared verification; confirmation does not waive Company's right to void the registration under §5.4 for matters later discovered. Company conducts and concludes verification acting reasonably and in good faith, and the §6.9 dispute window applies to any resulting Commission treatment.

5.3  Validity period.

An accepted Deal Registration is valid for ninety (90) days from acceptance. Company shall extend it for one additional ninety (90) day period on Partner's documented evidence of active engagement during the initial period. If the registration is neither converted nor extended, it expires at the end of the then-current period (a maximum of one hundred eighty (180) days from acceptance).

5.4  Disqualifiers.

A Deal Registration is void, and any Commission accrued is forfeited, where any of the following is true:

  1. Self-referral. Partner or any Affiliate of Partner is the end Customer or the beneficiary of the referred purchase;
  2. Conflicted insider referral. Partner is, at the time of registration, an employee, officer, or director of the referred entity, or a contractor or advisor to it whose referral would conflict with that engagement or breach the referred entity's policies. A contractor or advisor whose engagement permits the referral, and for whom no such conflict exists, is not disqualified under this subsection and may be credited as any other Partner;
  3. Pre-existing pipeline. The referred entity has an active opportunity, named contact, or unexpired trial with Company within ninety (90) days prior to the registration;
  4. Post-hoc registration. The registration was submitted after the referred entity independently contacted Company or initiated a trial;
  5. Identity unverifiable. Partner cannot complete identity, tax, or sanctions verification at the time of payout;
  6. Fraud or misrepresentation. The registration contains materially false statements or was procured by fraudulent means.

Email domain of the submitting individual is not a disqualifier. Personal email addresses are accepted where Partner has otherwise satisfied enrollment and verification requirements.

5.5  Co-sell.

Where a Deal Registration overlaps with a pre-existing Company opportunity under §5.4(c), Company may, at its sole discretion, offer a co-sell arrangement at a reduced Commission rate. Partner may accept or decline; declining releases the registration.

5.6  First-in-time.

Where two or more partners register the same Customer, attribution belongs to the first to submit a valid registration that Company accepts, and Company pays Commission solely to that Partner. Any arrangement to share Commission with another partner is solely between the partners concerned; Company is not a party to it, will not split or administer payments, and discharges its payment obligation in full by paying the attributed Partner.

5.7  Re-attribution on voided registration.

Company's acceptance of a Deal Registration does not cure any defect that makes it void under §5.4, and a registration may be found void after acceptance. If an accepted registration is later determined to be void under §5.4, it is treated as never having been valid for purposes of §5.6: attribution and any Commission shift to the next-earliest valid, accepted registration for the same Customer (if any), and any Commission paid or accrued on the voided registration is forfeited and subject to recovery under §6.11. Whether information is materially false, and all questions of validity, materiality, and timing under §5.4 and §5.6, are determined by Company acting reasonably and in good faith; Partner may dispute the resulting Commission treatment under §6.9. An honest, immaterial error does not void a registration, and a later registration does not displace an earlier valid one merely by being more complete or accurate.

6Fees & payment

6.1  Commission.

Subject to this §6 and Exhibit A, Company shall pay Partner Commission on each Qualified Referral. Commission is calculated as a percentage of the recognized year-one commissionable revenue from the Customer attributable to that referral, as defined in §6.4.

6.2  Qualification cliff.

Commission on a Qualified Referral becomes payable only after the Customer has been active and has paid Company for at least thirty (30) consecutive days following its subscription start date (the "Qualification Cliff"). Commission attributable to periods before the Qualification Cliff is forfeited if the Customer cancels or refunds.

6.3  Earning window.

Partner shall earn Commission on a Qualified Referral for the first twelve (12) months of the Customer's subscription. A separate loyalty residual may apply per Exhibit A.

6.4  Calculation basis.

Commission is calculated on the discounted contract value actually invoiced to and paid by the Customer, net of refunds, credits, and taxes, comprising both (a) the Product subscription and (b) recurring success-plan fees, notwithstanding that success plans are sold and invoiced as a separate service rather than as part of the subscription. Commission is not paid on one-time or non-recurring charges — including one-time support fees, professional-services or consulting revenue, and implementation fees — or on third-party pass-through costs, unless expressly stated in Exhibit A.

6.5  Anti-stacking.

Where a Qualified Referral could qualify under more than one Track, the single highest applicable Commission rate applies. Loyalty residual does not run concurrently with year-one Commission.

6.6  Discount discipline.

Deals discounted more than fifteen percent (15%) off Company's then-current list pricing and below one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000) in year-one ARR shall revert to the Referral-Track rate for Partner's tier, regardless of Partner's primary Track. Deals with year-one ARR of $100,000 or more retain Partner's primary-Track rate regardless of discount. This protects against compounding margin compression on smaller, deeply discounted deals.

6.7  Effective ceiling.

Total partner economics on any single Customer subscription line — Commission, loyalty residual, co-sell credit, and co-marketing credit combined — shall not exceed thirty-three percent (33%) of year-one recognized revenue from that subscription.

6.8  Payment schedule.

Company shall issue Partner a Commission statement within fifteen (15) days following each calendar quarter end. Payment of undisputed amounts shall be made within forty-five (45) days following quarter end, in U.S. dollars (USD), by ACH, wire, or other mutually agreed method.

6.9  Dispute window.

Partner shall have fourteen (14) days from the issuance of a Commission statement to dispute its content in writing, by email to its designated partner manager. Statements not disputed within that window are deemed accepted.

6.10  Taxes & withholding.

Commission amounts are exclusive of taxes. Partner is solely responsible for all taxes payable on Commission received. Company may withhold from Commission any amount required by applicable tax law and shall remit such amounts to the relevant authority.

6.11  Setoff.

Company may set off against Commission payable to Partner any undisputed amount owed by Partner to Company or any amount recovered from a Customer by way of refund or chargeback on the underlying subscription.

6.12  No advances.

Company is not obligated to advance Commission against unpaid invoices or expected future revenue.

Company may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Partner (a) engages in fraud or gross misrepresentation; (b) is placed on a sanctions or denied-parties listing; (c) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy; or (d) engages in conduct that materially damages Company's reputation.

A.3  Loyalty residual.

For Customers continuously active for more than twelve (12) months and not subject to replacement attribution, Partner shall receive a three percent (3%) loyalty residual in year two of the subscription only, subject to §6.5 (anti-stacking). No residual or other Commission shall accrue in year three or any subsequent year of the subscription.

A.3.1  Renewal treatment.

At each renewal of a Customer's subscription, the contract value is treated as comprising two components: (i) the Renewed Portion, being the portion of the renewed subscription at the same plan edition and subscription scope (org connections and data-volume tier) as the immediately preceding term; and (ii) the Expansion Portion, being any incremental ARR layered on at renewal, including additional org connections, a higher data-volume tier, or an upgraded plan edition.

The Renewed Portion is at all times treated as a renewal and shall not be reclassified as a net-new deal. The 3% loyalty residual under §A.3 applies to the Renewed Portion in year two only and is the sole compensation payable on that portion.

The Expansion Portion is treated as a fresh Qualified Referral for purposes of §6, generating year-one Commission at Partner's then-current tier rate on the incremental ARR only, with its own twelve (12) month earning window measured from the activation date of the added subscription scope, and its own subsequent loyalty residual under §A.3 (year two only). The Renewed Portion and the Expansion Portion accrue Commission in parallel and independently.

A.3.2  Expansion commission conditions.

Commission on an Expansion Portion is payable only if both of the following are true at the time of expansion:

  1. Partner involvement. Partner was substantively involved in the expansion — including discovery, scoping, customer-facing conversations, or sell-in activity. Expansion that occurs inbound and without Partner involvement does not pay.
  2. Active-engagement floor for Solution-rate. To earn Commission on the Expansion Portion at Partner's Solution-track rate (rather than its Referral-track rate), Partner must be actively delivering implementation, configuration, or operational management services to the Customer at the time of expansion. Active engagement is evidenced by (a) a written Partner attestation submitted with the expansion registration, and (b) a one-line Customer confirmation captured during the renewal cycle. Absent either, the Expansion Portion pays at the Referral-track rate for Partner's tier.

A.3.3  Reactivation after lapse.

Lapses are uncommon and are handled as renewals, not new acquisitions. A restart of a lapsed Customer does not generate a second year-one Commission for any Partner that has already earned year-one Commission on that Customer; such a Partner, if still attributed and substantively involved in the win-back, continues to earn the 3% loyalty residual under §A.3 only. Where a Partner not previously compensated on the Customer sources the reactivation and is validly attributed under §5, Company may credit it as a new Qualified Referral. Company resolves any question of timing or attribution under this section in good faith.
