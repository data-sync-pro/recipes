Before you submit
  1. Domain matches entity. Avoid registering a parent company when the buying entity is a subsidiary — attribution follows the contracted entity.
  2. Run a duplicate check. Use the search above. If Company has an active opportunity, you'll usually get a co-sell proposal under §5.5 rather than a rejection.
  3. One contact, named. Group aliases (info@, ops@) do not pass identity verification at payout (§5.4(e)).
  4. Track choice locks at acceptance. Switching from Referral to Solution later requires re-registration and may forfeit attribution date.
