00 Foreword

Why we wrote this.

Most partner programs are a contract and a hope. This one is documented end-to-end so partners know exactly what they're signing up for — how attribution works, when commissions clear, what disqualifies a referral, and what we owe each other.

Data Sync Pro replaces large chunks of custom Salesforce code with rules. We sell into the same enterprise orgs you already work with — RevOps leaders, Salesforce architects, integration partners. If you have a relationship with one, you can almost certainly turn it into a placed deal.

The pages that follow are written to be readable, not legalistic. The binding agreement is the Partner Agreement you sign at enrollment; everything here mirrors and explains it. Where the two ever conflict, the Agreement governs.

01 Chapter

The program at a glance.

A short summary you can show your finance team in two minutes.

Who it's for

Salesforce consultancies, RevOps advisors, ISV partners, system integrators, and independent operators with reach into Salesforce-using companies.

What you earn

Recurring commission on referred subscriptions for the first 12 months, with tiered rates that step up with volume.

When it clears

After a 30-day qualification cliff and once the referred customer has paid their first invoice. Paid quarterly thereafter.

What we provide

A registration portal, partner manager, trial licenses for proof-of-concept, co-branded materials, and a deal desk that responds within one business day.

The promise, in one sentence.

If you bring us a customer we wouldn't have reached on our own, we'll credit you fairly, pay you on time, and never compete with you on accounts you've registered.

02 Chapter

Partner tracks.

Two tracks — Referral and Solution. Both work the same way: you bring the customer, we hold the paper and bill them directly, and we pay you a commission on the subscriptions you drive. Referral is open to every partner; Solution you qualify for once, by proving you can implement. On any single deal you earn just one rate — whichever track fits what you did and qualify for. Commissions never stack.

Track A

Referral

You make an introduction. We close.

  • Submit a deal registration with prospect contact + context.
  • Hand off to our sales team after the warm intro.
  • No sales motion, demo, or implementation expected from you.
Base rate 15%
Track B

Solution

You sell, scope, and implement.

  • Lead the sales process — discovery and product demos — using training and materials we provide.
  • Deliver implementation services to the customer directly.
  • Be a registered company — not an individual.
  • Demonstrated Salesforce implementation experience.
Base rate 20%
Specialization

Technology / ISV

Building a product on DSP isn't a separate commission track — it's a designation you add to Referral or Solution. Your customers license DSP directly from us, and you earn the applicable rate on the subscriptions you drive. Building on DSP unlocks the technical benefits below.

  • Discounted licenses for dev, demo & test orgs to build and showcase on.
  • Listing and documentation support for your published integration.
  • API & roadmap access for partners building on the platform.
  • Technical co-marketing and joint go-to-market entitlements.
Which track fits you

Most partners should start with Referral — make the introduction, we do the rest. Choose Solution if you'll sell and implement yourself. Building a product on DSP? That's the Technology / ISV specialization above — you earn the Referral or Solution rate on the DSP subscriptions you drive, plus builder benefits.

03 Chapter

Becoming a partner.

Eligibility is about identity and accountability — not company size or deal volume.

Who can join.

  1. 1An identifiable legal person or entity. Individuals, LLCs, partnerships, or corporations. Anonymous applicants and unverifiable identities are declined.
  2. 2Able to execute the Partner Agreement. You sign on behalf of yourself or with authority for an entity.
  3. 3Able to accept payment in USD. Payouts are made in US dollars. Partners invoicing in other currencies are welcome — conversion is handled on your side, or contact us about local-currency arrangements.
  4. 4Not on a sanctions or denied-parties list. Standard screening at enrollment and annually thereafter.

What we explicitly don't require.

  • ×A corporate email domain to apply — though a company address is preferred over a personal one (gmail, outlook, proton, etc.) where you have one.
  • ×A minimum company size, revenue, or headcount.
  • ×A prior pipeline or signed deal as a precondition.

04 Chapter

Deal registration & attribution.

Attribution is the part of every partner program that breaks down first. We've designed it to be deterministic.

What gets registered

A specific company (legal entity, domain, or named opportunity) plus a primary contact — name, title, business email or phone.

Who is credited

The first partner to submit a valid registration for that company that we accept. Acceptance happens within two business days.

How long it holds

90 days from acceptance, with one 90-day extension on documented active engagement. After 180 days inactive, the registration expires.

Conflict resolution

If a registered account is already in our active sales pipeline, we tell you within two business days. Where you can add genuine value, we offer a co-sell at a reduced rate; otherwise we decline the registration.

What disqualifies a registration.

A registration is rejected — and any commission is forfeited — when any of the following are true. These exist to protect the program's integrity, not to claw back fairly-earned commissions.

DisqualifierWhy
Self-referral. The partner, or any entity owned or controlled by the partner, is the end customer. A referral commission compensates the introduction of a third party. Self-referral is a disguised discount — handled via our customer pricing, not the partner program.
Conflicted insider referral. The partner is an individual — an employee of the referred entity, or a contractor or advisor to it — whose referral would conflict with their engagement or breach the customer's policies. A referral that conflicts with the partner's duties to the customer isn't eligible. A contractor whose engagement permits the referral can still be credited like any other partner.
Already in pipeline. The named account has an active opportunity — or a prior sales conversation with someone there (a call, meeting, or email exchange) — within the past 90 days. The partner did not source the relationship. We'll tell you within two business days and offer a co-sell where appropriate.
Post-hoc registration. Submitted after the prospect has independently contacted us or started a trial. Attribution requires the registration to precede first contact.
Identity unverifiable. Partner cannot complete tax / KYC checks at payout time. We can't legally pay an unverified party.
05 Chapter

Commission structure.

Paid per transaction, starting with your first qualified referral.

The base mechanic.

  1. 1Per-deal, from deal one. Commission is earned on every qualified referral that converts, starting with your first.
  2. 2Recurring for 12 months. You earn the applicable rate on the customer's recurring revenue for the first 12 months after their start date — the Data Sync Pro subscription plus any recurring success plan (a separately billed service). It excludes one-time support, implementation, consulting, and other professional services.
  3. 330-day cliff. Commission becomes payable once the customer has been active and paid for 30 consecutive days.
  4. 4No cap. There is no per-deal or per-partner annual ceiling.

Tiered rates.

Your rate is set by your trailing-12-month referred ARR — a rolling window, not a calendar-year figure and not a running total since you joined. It's always the sum of your qualified referrals from the most recent 12 months: new deals lift it, and deals older than 12 months roll off, so your tier can move up or down over time. Tier changes apply to new commissions earned after the change; previously earned commissions are not retroactively adjusted.

Tier is measured at the partner level, not the deal level — and the metric is track-agnostic. Trailing-12-month referred ARR is simply the recognized annual subscription dollars across all your qualified referrals in the window, whether each one closed as a Referral or a Solution deal. Mixed-type deals are not split or weighted; a $100k Referral and a $100k Solution deal both contribute their full $100k to one combined ARR figure. That combined number sets your tier (the row); each individual deal's track sets which rate you read (the column). So a deal's type never changes how it counts toward your tier — only how it's paid.

Tier Trailing-12mo referred ARR Referral rate Solution rate
Registered $0 – $250k 15% 20%
Silver $250k – $1M 18% 23%
Gold $1M – $5M 22% 27%
Platinum $5M+ 25% 30%
Reading the table with mixed deal types

Sum every qualified referral from the last 12 months — Referral and Solution alike — into one ARR figure; that picks your row. Then read each deal in the column for the track it closed under. A Silver partner with both deal types reads 18% on their Referral deals and 23% on their Solution deals — same row, different columns.

Renewals & expansions.

At every renewal, Data Sync Pro splits the contract into two components and treats each on its own terms.

Renewed subscription. The portion of the contract that is simply being renewed at the same level — same plan edition, same org connections and data-volume tier — is a renewal, never reclassified as net-new. The original partner receives no automatic year-one commission on this portion. Instead, the partner earns a 3% loyalty residual on the renewed value in year two only — paid automatically, with no action required, as long as the customer stays continuously active and attribution hasn't transferred to another partner. After year two, the residual ends; no further commission accrues on the renewed portion regardless of how many additional years the customer stays.

Net-new expansion. Any incremental value layered on at renewal time — additional org connections, a higher data-volume tier, or an upgraded plan edition — is treated as a fresh year-one deal on the incremental ARR only. The partner earns their then-current tier rate on that increment, with its own 12-month earning window measured from the activation date of the added subscription scope. A new 3% year-two residual attaches to that increment when its year two comes around. The renewed portion continues on renewal economics in parallel.

Worked example

A Silver Solution partner's customer renews from $120k to $180k. The $120k renewed portion pays the partner a $3,600 residual in year two (3% × $120k) and nothing after. The $60k added portion pays $13,800 in year-one commission (23% Silver Solution × $60k), with its own 12-month window and its own future 3% residual. Total partner payout that year: $17,400.

Two guardrails apply to the expansion portion:

  1. APartner-involvement test. To claim expansion commission, the partner registers the expansion during the renewal cycle — a lightweight expansion registration on the existing account, no conflict or pipeline check needed. Registration alone isn't enough to earn, but you don't need to run a full discovery either: any genuine hand in the expansion — discovery, scoping, a customer conversation, or sell-in — qualifies for the Referral-track rate at your tier. Only pure inbound growth the partner had nothing to do with is excluded. Earning the higher Solution rate takes the additional delivery test in (B).
  2. BActive-engagement floor for Solution-rate. To collect the Solution-tier rate (rather than the Referral rate) on the expansion tranche, the partner must still be actively delivering implementation, configuration, or operational management to the customer at the time of expansion. Otherwise the expansion pays at the Referral-track rate for the partner's tier. This is verified by partner attestation plus a one-line customer confirmation captured during the renewal cycle.

Stacking, ceilings & discount discipline.

A few rules to keep commission costs predictable and to prevent unintended combinations from compounding on a single deal.

  1. 1No stacking across tracks. If a deal qualifies under more than one track (e.g. Referral + Solution), the single highest applicable rate applies — never the sum. The deal desk picks the track at close.
  2. 2No stacking with loyalty residual. Year-one commission and the 3% loyalty residual cannot run simultaneously on the same subscription line. Residual begins only after the 12-month earning window closes.
  3. 3Co-sell & co-marketing credit. Any co-sell or co-marketing credit on a deal counts toward — and is capped by — the effective ceiling in §6; it is never paid on top of base commission.
  4. 4Discount discipline. Two things. First, your commission is always figured on the actual discounted price the customer pays — not the list price. Second, if a deal is discounted more than 15% off list and is under $100k in year-one ARR, it pays at the Referral-track rate for your tier even if you're a Solution partner — a deep discount on a smaller deal already thins our margin, so we don't layer the higher Solution rate on top. Larger deals (year-one ARR of $100k or more) keep your Solution rate regardless of discount. (Example: a Solution partner who closes an $80k deal at 20% off list earns the Referral rate on that deal; the same 20% discount on a $150k deal keeps the Solution rate.)
  5. 5Multi-year commitments. For prepaid multi-year contracts, year-one commission applies only to the year-one ARR portion. The loyalty residual covers year two only; later years pay nothing.
  6. 6Effective ceiling. Total partner economics on any single deal — base commission, residual, and any co-sell or co-marketing credit — will not exceed 33% of recognized year-one ARR.

Payouts, taxes & reporting.

No mystery. Quarterly payout, dashboard you can audit, statements you can hand your accountant.

Schedule.

  • Commission period — calendar quarter (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec).
  • Statement issued — within 15 days of period close.
  • Dispute window — 14 days from statement to flag discrepancies.
  • Payment — within 45 days of period close, in US dollars by ACH, wire, or supported alternative.

What we need from you.

  • Completed W-9 (US) or W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E (non-US) on file.
  • Verified bank or payment-platform details.
  • For entities: tax ID and registered business address.
  • For individuals: government-issued ID at first payout above $5,000 cumulative.

Reporting you'll have access to.

Pipeline view

Every registered account, status, and expected close.

Earnings ledger

Per-deal accrual, cliff status, paid & unpaid balances.

Tier tracker

Trailing-12-month ARR and projected tier transition date.

Tax documents

Year-end 1099-NEC or equivalent, downloadable by January 31.

The list of things partners must do — and must not do — to stay in the program.

  • Disclose your partner relationship when it could materially affect a customer's decision.
  • Honor registrations made by other partners — no end-running another partner's deal.
  • Promptly report security incidents that affect any data we share with you.
  • Maintain at least one trained contact who has completed our partner enablement.

  • Make claims about performance, security, or compliance that are not in our official materials.
  • Use Data Sync Pro trademarks or screenshots without written approval.
  • Cold-spam prospects under our name or imply employment by Data Sync Pro.
  • Bid on our brand keywords in paid search.
  • Share confidential roadmap, pricing exceptions, or customer data with third parties.

Indemnification & legal defense.

If a partner's own actions trigger a third-party claim against Data Sync Pro, the partner stands behind it. In plain terms, the partner agrees to defend Data Sync Pro and cover the resulting claims, damages, and reasonable legal costs when they arise from: misrepresenting the product beyond our published materials; breaching this Agreement or applicable law; unauthorized use of our trademarks; or the partner's handling of a customer's systems or data. In turn, Data Sync Pro defends the partner against any claim that the Data Sync Pro product itself, used as authorized, infringes a third party's intellectual property. Each side notifies the other promptly of a claim and cooperates on the defense. The binding terms — including limits and procedure — are set in the Partner Agreement; this is the plain-English summary.

09 Chapter

Confidentiality, IP & data.

Short version: assume everything we share with you is confidential unless we mark it otherwise. Treat customer data with the same care we do.

Confidential information.

Roadmap, pricing exceptions, customer lists, internal performance benchmarks, and any unreleased product information. Survives termination of the agreement for three years.

Trademarks & brand use.

You may identify yourself as a Data Sync Pro partner using the partner badge provided in your portal. Other uses of our name, logo, or screenshots require written approval — granted quickly when the use case is fair.

Customer data.

If a customer gives you access to their Salesforce org — including the Data Sync Pro installed within it — that access is governed by the customer's own terms, not this Agreement. You are responsible for following the customer's instructions and applicable data-protection law.

Feedback you give us.

Any feedback, suggestions, enhancement ideas, or bug reports you share are non-confidential, and we may use them freely — without attribution or compensation. Anything we develop from that feedback — product enhancements, improvements, or fixes — is owned entirely by Data Sync Pro; you gain no ownership, license, or residual rights in it. We'll still usually credit good ideas anyway.

10 Chapter

Term, termination & disputes.

How it ends — and what happens to commissions in flight.

Term.

The agreement is annual and auto-renews. Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days' notice, or immediately for material breach uncured after 14 days' notice.

What survives termination.

  1. 1Commissions on already-closed deals continue to be paid for the remainder of their 12-month earning window.
  2. 2Active registrations with documented engagement remain credited if they close within 90 days of termination.
  3. 3Confidentiality obligations survive for three years.
  4. 4Termination for cause (fraud, gross misrepresentation, sanctions violation) forfeits unpaid commissions.

Disputes.

Statement discrepancies are flagged within the 14-day dispute window. We respond in writing within 10 business days. Unresolved matters proceed to good-faith mediation, then binding arbitration in the governing jurisdiction set in the Partner Agreement.

Appendix.

A.1  Glossary

Qualified referral
A registered deal that has been accepted, not disqualified under §04, and has resulted in a paying customer past the 30-day cliff.
Trailing-12-month referred ARR
Sum of recognized annual recurring revenue from your qualified referrals over the most recent 12 calendar months — counted across all deals regardless of track. It sets your tier (the rate row); each deal's track sets the rate column.
Cliff
The 30-day post-payment period before commission on a deal becomes payable.
Co-sell
An engagement model where a registered deal overlaps with an existing pipeline. The partner receives a reduced commission in exchange for shared execution.
Loyalty residual
A 3% commission on year two of a referred customer's subscription, contingent on continuous activity and no replacement attribution. Does not extend to year three or beyond.

A.2  Quick FAQ

If a potential customer refers themselves, are they eligible for a partner commission?

No. A self-referral, or a referral to any entity the partner owns or controls, is excluded under §04. Customers seeking a direct purchase discount should ask our sales team — that's a separate mechanism.

I only have a personal email address. Can I be a partner?

Yes — a personal email address won't disqualify you. We do prefer a company domain where you have one, but you'll complete the same identity verification either way.

Do I have to hit N successful referrals before I'm paid?

No. We pay per transaction starting with your first qualified referral. There is no threshold.

What if the customer churns in month two?

Commission already paid is not clawed back. Commissions not yet paid (months three onward) stop accruing.

A customer of mine lapsed and later comes back. Do I earn again?

Lapses are rare with DSP, so we keep this simple: a win-back is treated as a renewal, not a new deal. You won't be paid a second year-one commission on a customer you've already earned year-one on — but if you're still the attributed partner and helped win them back, you keep earning the 3% loyalty residual. The only time a full year-one applies is when a different partner, never paid on that customer, actually sources the win-back. Anything unusual, we sort out with you in good faith.

Can two partners share credit on one deal?

DSP pays a single partner — the first to submit a valid registration we accept. If you want to split with another partner, that's an arrangement between the two of you; we pay the attributed partner and stay out of it.

Can I partner with a competing product?

Yes. Exclusivity is not required. We will, however, scope what confidential information you receive accordingly.
